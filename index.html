Plant City, Florida · 1980 – 1984

THE RAIDER ROOM

Named for the horse-mounted Raider up the road at Plant City High — painted right onto the roofline, next to Pac-Man's dots. A family-run game room, and the place I grew up in.

A Few Photos That Survived

Between 1980 and 1984, my parents ran a game room called the Raider Room. I was twelve to sixteen years old, and most afternoons you could find me somewhere in these thirteen photographs — behind the counter, propped against the Defender cabinet, or just out of frame.

These are the only photos that made it. Click any of them to look closer.
